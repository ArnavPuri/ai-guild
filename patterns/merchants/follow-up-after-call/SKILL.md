# Follow-up After a Call

Send the follow-up while the call is fresh, short enough to read on a phone.

## Ask first

If missing: the call notes, who was on the call and their roles, and what the seller wants the next step to be.

## Steps

1. Pull out the buyer's top two or three priorities from the notes, using their own words where the notes have them.
2. Note anything the seller promised to send or do, with dates.
3. Note open questions or concerns the buyer raised.
4. Draft the email: a one-line thank-you tied to something specific from the call; the priorities as a short list ("What I heard"); what the seller will do and by when; one clear ask with a proposed date.
5. Write a subject line that names the buyer's goal, not the product.
6. Keep the email under 150 words.

## Output

Subject line, then the email, ready to send. Then, separately, a two-line internal note for the seller's records: deal stage and next step.

## Check before replying

- Every priority in the email appears in the notes.
- Exactly one ask, with a date or a choice of dates.
- No attachments or materials promised that the notes do not mention.
