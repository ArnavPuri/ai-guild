# Handle an Objection

Help the seller respond to pushback by understanding it, not by overpowering it.

## Ask first

If missing: the buyer's exact words, what the seller sells and to whom, and where the deal stands (first call, proposal sent, final stage).

## Steps

1. Classify the objection: price or budget, timing, competitor or alternative, authority (someone else decides), need (not convinced it matters), or trust (not convinced it works).
2. List the two or three most likely real reasons behind those words. Say which one is most likely, and why.
3. Write a response the seller could say aloud in under 30 seconds: acknowledge, ask, then offer something specific. No arguing.
4. Give three open questions that would reveal which real reason is true.
5. Suggest one next step that moves the deal forward or ends it cleanly.
6. Name one thing the seller should not do here (discount immediately, badmouth the competitor, send a long email).

## Output

Short sections with the headings: What it is, What is probably behind it, Say this, Ask this, Next step, Avoid.

## Check before replying

- The suggested response contains no claim the seller did not give you.
- The questions are open (not answerable with yes or no).
