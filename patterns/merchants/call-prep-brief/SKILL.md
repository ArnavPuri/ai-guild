# Call Prep Brief

Walk into the call knowing what you want out of it and what you need to learn.

## Ask first

If missing: the company and what it does, who is on the call and their roles, the stage of the deal, notes from earlier conversations, and what the seller sells.

## Steps

1. State the single goal for this call, as an outcome: "agree a pilot scope", "meet the budget holder", not "have a good chat".
2. Summarise what is already known in five bullets or fewer: the problem, why now, who decides, budget, timeline. Mark each unknown as **Unknown**.
3. Write five open discovery questions that fill the unknowns, most important first.
4. For each person on the call, one line on what they probably care about, based only on their role and what the seller told you.
5. List the two most likely objections, with a one-line response to each.
6. Propose the next step to ask for at the end of the call.

## Output

One page with the headings: Goal, What we know, Questions, People, Likely objections, Ask for.

## Check before replying

- Nothing is stated about the company or people that the seller did not give you; unknowns are marked as unknown.
- The questions are open, not yes or no.
