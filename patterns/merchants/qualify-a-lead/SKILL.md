# Qualify a Lead

Decide quickly whether a lead deserves time now, later or not at all, and reply well either way.

## Ask first

If missing: the lead's message or form details, what the seller sells, and who their best customers are (size, industry, problem).

## Steps

1. Compare the lead with the ideal customer: say how it matches and where it does not, in two or three bullets.
2. Check the four basics from the evidence given: **Problem** (is there a real pain?), **Authority** (is this person able to decide or influence?), **Budget** (any signal they can pay?), **Timing** (any reason to act soon?). Mark each Yes, Partly, No or Unknown.
3. Give an overall rating: Hot (talk this week), Warm (nurture and qualify further), or Not a fit, with one sentence of reasoning.
4. Recommend the next action.
5. Draft the first reply. For Hot and Warm leads: thank them, show you read their message, ask one or two questions that fill the biggest unknown, and offer a time. For Not a fit: a polite, helpful no, with a pointer to what might suit them better if you know it.

## Output

Fit, The basics, Rating, Next action, then the reply ready to send (under 120 words).

## Check before replying

- The rating follows from the evidence, not from the company's name or size alone.
- The reply asks no more than two questions.
