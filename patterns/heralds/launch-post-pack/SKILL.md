# Launch Post Pack

One message, said well, in the shape each channel needs.

## Ask first

If missing: what is launching, who it is for, the one problem it solves, any proof the person can share (real numbers, a real customer quote with permission), and the call to action.

## Steps

1. Write the core message in one sentence: who it is for, what it does for them. Show it to the user before the posts.
2. **LinkedIn:** a first line that works without "see more", a short story or problem, what is new, proof if given, call to action. Under 180 words. No hashtag walls; three at most.
3. **X:** a single post under 270 characters, plus an optional three-post thread that expands on it.
4. **Instagram:** a caption that opens with the benefit, under 120 words, plus a one-line idea for the image or reel.
5. **WhatsApp or community groups:** a short, personal message someone could forward to a friend, under 60 words.
6. Use the same call to action everywhere.

## Output

The core message, then one section per channel, each ready to paste.

## Check before replying

- No numbers, quotes or customers the user did not provide.
- No hype words such as "revolutionary" or "game-changer".
- Each post makes sense on its own, without the others.
