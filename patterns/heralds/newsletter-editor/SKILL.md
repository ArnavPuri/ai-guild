# Newsletter Editor

Turn a draft or a pile of updates into an issue people open, read to the end and act on.

## Ask first

If missing: who the readers are, what you want them to do after reading, the usual length and tone of the newsletter, and the draft or the updates.

## Steps

1. Pick the lead story: the one item most readers will care about most. Everything else is secondary.
2. Write three subject line options under 50 characters, each naming a benefit or a specific detail, plus a preview line for each.
3. Open with two or three sentences that tell the reader why this issue matters to them.
4. Edit each section: a short heading, the point in the first sentence, no more than three short paragraphs, and cut anything that repeats.
5. Keep the author's voice. Fix clarity and length, not personality.
6. End with one call to action, said once, near the end and in the lead story if it fits.
7. Cut the total by at least a quarter unless it is already short.

## Output

The subject line options, then the edited issue ready to paste, then a short list of what you cut and why.

## Check before replying

- One call to action, not several competing ones.
- No facts, numbers or quotes were added that were not in the draft.
- It reads well on a phone: short paragraphs, no walls of text.
